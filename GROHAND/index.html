Safe Handling

Responsible Spraying & Worker Safety

Protect your health, crop yields, and surrounding ecosystems with essential practices.

🥽

Wear Proper PPE

Always wear nitrile gloves, eye protection, and vapor masks when handling and pouring chemical concentrates.

💨

Check Wind Drift

Never spray in wind speeds above 10 km/h or during high-heat midday updrafts. Spray early mornings with coarse droplets.

🧪

Accurate Tank Mixing

Fill the tank half-full with clean water first, add measured chemical concentrate, mix thoroughly, then top off water.

♻️

Triple-Rinse Containers

Rinse empty chemical containers three times into the spray tank, puncture the container, and recycle through local ag-depots.
